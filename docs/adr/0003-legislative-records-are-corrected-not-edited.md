# Legislative records are corrected, not edited, once a session is held

A session goes Draft → Scheduled → Held. Until it is Held, its order of business, attendance and outcomes can be edited freely. After that, and for stage history at any time, a mistake is fixed by adding a correction entry with a reason, and the original entry is never changed or deleted. These records are the official account of what the SB did, so they must show what was recorded and what was later changed, by whom and why, not just the latest value.

## Consequences

Stage history, attendance and votes are append-only after a session is Held; the current value is the latest entry. Every change and correction is also written to the audit log.
