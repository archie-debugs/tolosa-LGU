# SB members are a roster, separate from user accounts

Attendance, votes and committee chairs refer to a member roster (name, position, term), not to user accounts. A member can optionally be linked to a login. Not every member has an account, the "Secretary / Vice Mayor" role maps to an Employee account, and accounts are deactivated or reassigned while the legislative record must keep showing who attended and voted.

## Consequences

Members are never deleted, only ended with their term, so past sessions keep pointing at them. Adding a person to the SB means adding a member, and only optionally a user account.
